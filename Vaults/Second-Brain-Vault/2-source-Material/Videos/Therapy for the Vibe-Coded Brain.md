# Therapy for the Vibe-Coded Brain

Vibe coding is pretty code for doing, but pretty terrible for learning.

Before the AI era, developers needed to google their problem first, ask a friend, ask on Stack Overflow, and pause for a moment to think about the problem. However, in the AI era, typing a prompt and reading an answer feels more productive than a long empty pause. Is it?

AI impairs your cognitive abilities, and annihilates your meta-cognitive functions.

The 5 sins of problem solving:
forming: right question wrong answer
dislodging: stuck in a rut(your approach isn't working and struggle to change it)
assumption: wrong question, right answer(you ain't solving the problem you're supposed to)
location: so close, but so far(you think you're done but you realize there are missing steps)
Achievement: Band-aid on a broken bone
(after introducing LLMs)
Progression: Falling behind without noticing(you think you solved it, but AI did)
Interruption: copilot autocomplete ahh interrupting your train of thoughts
Mislead: obvious

The goal isn't to avoid these sins, but to be aware of them.

Reflection:
These hurdles don't apply only to coding, but to using AI in general. For instance, I am pretty sure it applies to academic writing in the same way.
