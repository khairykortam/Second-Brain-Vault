# Odysseas

Make an essay the unit of your notes. It's the medium that  you express your ideas in. Try to use your own words, don't quote too much or use the text you're reading verbatim. In short, the more effort you make, the more you learn. Essays help you achieve this goal.

A mini-essay is simply a paragraph; I don't know why he uses this obfuscated term.

The Essay should convey just one idea: modular. 

Divide your essay into an intro(hook), body, and a conclusion.
