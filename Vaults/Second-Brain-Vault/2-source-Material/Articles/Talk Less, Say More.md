# Talk Less, Say More

Talkaholism is the urge to speak and act on this urge even if one knows it will hurt them.
I have a simple exercise: when you have the possibility to say nothing, say nothing.

Sometimes you have to just ask some good questions, listen to the person speaking, and try to actively listen to them and ask them further questions rather than just responding to them with your opinion.

Try to use a "yes, and..." scheme for responses
