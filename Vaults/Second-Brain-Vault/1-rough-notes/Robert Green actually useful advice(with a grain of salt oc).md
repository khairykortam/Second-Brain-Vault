# Robert Green actually useful advice(with a grain of salt oc)

- Don't overspeak.
- Care about reputation, try to flourish your reputation and things will go towards the better.
- try to avoid the unhappy/unfortunate.
- try to be persuasive by appealing to people's self interest.
- Kinda a good advice to not be in the scene 24/7, as you kinda become a background character anyways? So, withdraw more often, but only after you're established
- Do not completely withdraw.
- Do not offend the wrong person
- Do not commit to anyone/any side 
- Play the virgin queen: give them hope but never satisfaction
- don't let people drag you into their petty fights and squabbles. Seem interested and supportive, but remain neutral. let the others do the fight while you stand back
- play dumb
- Concentrate your efforts
- don't let others define you; define yourself
- Don't not act on hesitation
- Don't show off how hard you work
- The illusion of choice: make others feel that they can choose while they don't
- exploit others weaknesses, but don't be toxic
- to appear common will make you disrespected, try to higher yourself up by acting confident of your powers(overconfidence is sometimes not that bad but don't go too far)
- stay calm
- nothing is often free
- Strike the shephered and the sheep will scatter
- ovrall, try to calculate motives, weaknesses, causes, and goals, but don't be toxic; don't overanalyze things.
