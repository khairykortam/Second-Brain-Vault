**Capstone projects last year, which team workflow approach do you prefer: specializing in a single task throughout the entire project lifecycle, or rotating tasks among group members? Justify your choice using four distinct reasons.

Personal Reflection/Team Collaboration

  
  

Having finished 2 capstone projects, I gained experience in capstone. During the process of making capstone, one experimented with multiple workflows. Therefore, for me, I prefer certain approaches as they generate better results or have more efficacy. Specifically, I prefer each team member to specialize in a specific task. This lies in four distinct reasons: labour division makes hard tasks simpler, specialisation eases team management, modularity generates higher efficiency, and it’s more time efficient.

  

First, breaking hard tasks into simpler, modular parts and assigning each part to a specific team member makes navigating hard tasks easier for that specific team member, and thus increases the overall efficiency of the whole team. For instance, a team member cannot navigate a task of identifying the whole problem at once; however, he can easily finish a task of just getting 5 research papers about possible locations and sharing this idea with the team. Therefore, when each member’s work is clear, identifiable and quantifiable, it removes the ambiguity of the process. Second, as formerly mentioned, specialization makes team management easier since each member knows his own role in the project, and therefore it removes the confusion that results from dividing each task among all members. Third, specialization generates higher efficiency as each person has better expertise in his own work than the other team member, so instead of making all the team members navigate the tasks with low efficiencies, a specialized person can handle the same task with a higher efficiency. Lastly, specialization is time efficient. For instance, if I am assigned to make a 3D model while I don’t have any experience in 3D modeling, I may take considerable time learning the tool at first, even before engaging in the main work. Therefore, a team mate specialized in 3D modeling will consume less time to do the same work. 

  

Ultimately, specializing in a specific task in capstone makes team work easier, which is a clear application of the proverb “divide and conquer."

  
  
  

Each stage of the Engineering Design Process (EDP) has specific criteria for success. What are three essential requirements for successfully completing the problem-identification stage of the EDP? Explain each requirement.

Using the Engineering Design Process (EDP)

  
  

The EDP(the engineering design process) is a systematic, iterative process. It requires consistency and precision to obtain a valid, optimal solution. Furthermore, the quality of each step depends on the previous one. Hence, the most important step is identifying the problem stage. And to execute this phase optimally, three qualities are essential: doing background research, specifying the project’s requirements, and choosing ideas critically.

  

First, doing background research helps us narrow the area of discovery. That is, it makes us know the features of the field: common terms used in food packaging, common methods, prior solutions, global food standards. Therefore, it makes identifying the problem easier. Second, specifying the project’s requirements makes the searching process clear, by having an identifiable goal. For example, to be able to identify the problematic crop, you must first specify what features you want to measure—CO2 emissions, bacterial or fungal growth rate, etc.— before you start the searching process. Therefore, if you identify the requirements clearly, the problem becomes more concise and clear. Lastly, during the search process, we can face multiple proposed problems to be solved. However, to decide between them, one must think critically of each of them: does this problem satisfy my requirements? Does this problem answer my questions? Can I practically apply a solution to this problem? This can guarantee that you get the best results possible.

  

Ultimately, the problem identification step is the most significant in the capstone project, as all the other steps rely on it, starting from the solution, to the exhibition.
