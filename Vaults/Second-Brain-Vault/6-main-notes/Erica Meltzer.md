# Chapter 4: Joining and Separating Sentences:
- To seperate complete sentences:
- 1) period or semicolon
- 2) comma + FANBOYS
- 3) colon or Dash
- NOT a comma alone
- . = ; = comma+FANBOYS
- : = — -> only when the second clause explains or describes a result of the first
- when used to begin a clause, (however, therefore, thus, consequently, moreover, nevertheless) must follow a period or emicolon, never a comma.
- comma + FANBOYS -> if not comma -> run-on
- when the subject is the same and not repeated in the second clause, do not use a comma.
- comma splice: 
- Shortcut: commas splices are often signaled by the construction comma + pronoun whenever you see this underlined, you should be on your guard
