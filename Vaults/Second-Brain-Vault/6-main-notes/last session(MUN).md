- pathos: does the writer appeal to the emotions of their reader?

1- starting with a metaphor or storytelling
2- Empathy in sharing information using exaggeration adverbs
3- Anecdote(narration)

- logos: does the writer appeal to the rational mind by using logic and evidence?
- The appeal of logic: target reader's reason or logic. 
- includes logical assumptions based on source
