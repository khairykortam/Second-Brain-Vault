# The Rationalism of René Descartes 
- "I swallowed a lot of falsehood; therefore, I must demolish everything and build anew"
- Descartes' Demon
- The first truth that can be known with certainty is one's own existence
- "whatever is clear and distinct is true"
- Some ideas come from external things ,other seem to be invented, other are innate
- The idea of perfection couldn't come from less-thant-perfect source(God)
- Some ideas are confused or obscure, and other are clear and distinct(geometry, math, abstract concepts, etc..) and can't be wrong.
- "cartesian circle": using clear and distinct ideas to prove themselves trustworthy
