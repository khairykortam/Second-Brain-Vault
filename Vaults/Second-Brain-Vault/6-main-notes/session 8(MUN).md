writing on sensitive topics
- political writing: political situation, formal, objective(3rd person), analytical, no metaphors.
- emotionlessness is safer.
- used in: 
- position paper: consists of the header that explains the committee(UNSC,UNHCR, UNW) representing the paper ( achieving the objective perspective of the political writing). 3 paragraphs explain the problem, UN action, proposed solutions, respectively
- moderated caucus: in informal discussion, papers of info to compete in conference
- official resolutions and doc: at end of conf.(unbiased)
- tips and tricks: 
1. be neutral, unbiased
2. be specific to avoid misunderstandings
3. focus on facts and avoid assumptions and actions that are supposed to happen, even if they're highly guaranteed
4. try hard to read from another person's perspective to check the sensitivity reached and avoid being excessive
